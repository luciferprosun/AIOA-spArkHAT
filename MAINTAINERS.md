## Adding maintainers

New maintainers may be invited after sustained constructive contribution, reliable review work, respect for security and authority boundaries, and explicit approval by the current maintainer.

Maintainer status does not grant authority to bypass the runtime's human-approval, credential, security, or provenance controls.

## Shared advancement policy

Each advance is a separately approved bounded experiment with immutable source/target SHAs, fresh fixture/security baseline, kill-switch owner and exact resource budget. No invented load numbers or SLA. Specify a reviewed concurrency/time cap before stress execution. Failures retain evidence and prevent advancing; return to last proven configuration without deleting history.

A durable transactional local store alone cannot prove external exactly-once. Target must support atomic idempotency/fencing or UNKNOWN effects remain held for reconciliation/manual decision. Serverless deployment and recurring resources remain blocked. After pre-freeze preparation, stop coding until explicit unlock.
